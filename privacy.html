Privacy Policy – Purrfect Jam

Last updated: 2026-10-21

Purrfect Jam ("the game") is a puzzle game for Android and iOS, published by Infinity Studio ("we"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you play.

In short

  • We do not run any servers for the game, have no accounts and do not collect your name, e-mail or any other personal details.
  • The game shows advertising from Google AdMob. Google, not us, collects the limited device information described below to show and measure ads.
  • Your game progress stays on your device.

What stays on your device

Your progress (current level, coins, chosen board look, sound setting, language, daily-gift streak, which tutorial lessons you have played, and whether your Purrfect Plus subscription was active the last time the game checked) is saved only on your device, in the game's own storage. We never receive it. If you delete the game, it is deleted with it. If device backup is turned on (Google account on Android, iCloud on iOS), the operating system may copy this data into your backup; we do not control or see that backup.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game contains rewarded videos (you choose to watch one to get a bonus) and full-screen ads between some levels. These are provided by Google AdMob. When an ad is requested, Google may collect and use, according to its own policies:

  • your device's advertising identifier (Advertising ID on Android, IDFA on iOS, only if you allow tracking on iOS),
  • your IP address and the approximate location derived from it,
  • device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version) and diagnostics,
  • how you interact with ads (shown, clicked, watched).

Google uses this to show ads (personalised ones only if you agree), to measure them and to prevent fraud. We do not receive this data and cannot identify you from it. More: How Google uses information from apps that use its services and Google Advertising Policies.

Your choices

  • Europe (EEA) and United Kingdom: the first time you open the game a consent screen from Google asks whether ads may be personalised. You can change your answer any time with the "Privacy & ads" button in the main menu.
  • iPhone / iPad: iOS asks whether the game may track you. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", only non-personalised ads are shown.
  • Android and iOS settings: you can reset or delete your advertising ID or turn personalised ads off in the system settings (Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads; iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking).
  • Not watching any rewarded video never limits the game.

Purrfect Plus subscription

The game offers an optional monthly subscription, Purrfect Plus, that removes the ads. The purchase is made and paid entirely through the Apple App Store or Google Play: we never see or store your name, card or payment details. The game only asks the store whether your subscription is active (so that it can switch the ads off and restore your subscription on a new phone). Manage or cancel the subscription at any time in your App Store or Google Play account settings. Members do not see ads and are not asked for ad consent. The game is fully playable without the subscription.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children. The game has no chat and no social features. The only real-money purchase is the optional Purrfect Plus subscription, handled by the app store.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. For data that Google handles for advertising, you can use the controls above and Google's own privacy tools (myaccount.google.com). You may contact us with any question.

Changes

If this policy changes (for example if a future version adds other purchases or online features), we will publish the new version here and in the store listing before it takes effect.
